|----------|-------------|
| LLM lexical-review output → `ProviderManager` | Untrusted model output enters trusted review flow |
| lexical-review service → `DeckBuilder` | Typed verdicts decide whether cards are accepted, corrected, or rejected |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-01 | T | `ankideck_generator/core/providers.py` | mitigate | Validate lexical-review responses with Pydantic `LexicalReviewResult` before returning them so malformed or reshaped AI output cannot spoof a valid verdict. |
| T-03-02 | R | lexical-review verdict contract | mitigate | Constrain verdicts to `accept/correct/reject` and require reason-code-bearing evidence fields so low-confidence outcomes are attributable and cannot silently become an implicit approval. |
| T-03-03 | I | losing-sense audit data | mitigate | Preserve `winning_sense` and `losing_sense_candidates` in typed results so ambiguity evidence is retained for queue artifacts and later tuning. |
</threat_model>
